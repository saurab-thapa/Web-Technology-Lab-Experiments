🥟 Delicious Momo Recipe

Your Complete Guide to Making Delicious Homemade Momos

About History Recipe Facts

About Momos

Momos are soft dumplings filled with vegetables, paneer or meat and are steamed until perfectly cooked. They are one of the most popular street foods in India and are enjoyed with spicy chutney.

History of Momos

Momos originated in Tibet and gradually became popular in Nepal, Bhutan and India. Today, they are one of the favorite snacks across the country. Different regions have introduced their own variations like fried, tandoori and cheese momos.

Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Flour
  • Cabbage
  • Carrot
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Oil

Preparation

  1. Prepare soft dough.
  2. Mix vegetables with spices.
  3. Roll small wrappers.
  4. Add filling.
  5. Seal the edges.
  6. Steam for 15 minutes.
  7. Serve with chutney.

Interesting Facts

  • Steamed momos are healthier than fried momos.
  • They are served with spicy tomato chutney.
  • Vegetable and chicken momos are the most popular.
  • Momos are loved all over India.
