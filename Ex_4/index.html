Welcome to Darjeeling

Nestled in the Himalayas of West Bengal, Darjeeling is famous for its lush tea gardens, breathtaking mountain views, colonial architecture, and the UNESCO World Heritage Toy Train.

History

Darjeeling was originally inhabited by the Lepcha people. During the 19th century, the British developed it into a hill station and tea plantation center. Today it is one of India's most popular tourist destinations and is globally known for its premium tea.

Traditions & Culture

Darjeeling has a rich blend of Nepali, Tibetan, Lepcha and Bengali cultures. Festivals like Losar, Dasain, Tihar and Buddha Purnima are celebrated with great enthusiasm. Traditional music, dance and colorful attire reflect the region's cultural diversity.
